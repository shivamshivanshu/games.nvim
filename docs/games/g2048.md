# 2048

Solo: slide the tiles of a 4×4 board one way at a time; two equal tiles that meet merge into their sum. Server: `server/games/g2048/`; board UI: `lua/games/games/g2048.lua`. The game type is `g2048`, because a Go package name cannot start with a digit; the catalogue names it "2048".

## Rules as implemented
- One seat, `solver`. Start with `:Games solo g2048`.
- The board starts with two tiles, each spawned as below.
- A move slides every tile as far as it goes up, down, left or right.
  - Two equal tiles that meet merge into one tile of their sum.
  - Each tile merges at most once per move: `2 2 4 ·` moved left gives `4 4 · ·`, not `8`.
  - Merging goes toward the move: of three equal tiles, the two nearest the edge they move to merge. `2 2 2 ·` moved left gives `4 2 · ·`; moved right, `· · 2 4`.
- A move that changes nothing is illegal. The view's `movable` lists the directions that would change the board.
- After each legal move one tile spawns on an empty cell: a 2 nine times in ten, else a 4. A legal move always leaves an empty cell.
- The score is the sum of every merged tile's value: merging two 8s scores 16.
- Making a 2048 for the first time sets `reached` in the view and sends a `reached` event. Play goes on.
- When no move can change the board, the game ends, reason `no_moves`. It is a win if a 2048 was ever made, else a loss.

## Randomness
- The start tiles come from the game's seed, and each spawn from the seed and the step number: the core's seeded rng, as for ludo and poker. A restart replays every step and gets the same tiles; the restore test checks this.
- A spawn draws the cell first, uniformly among the empty cells in row-major order, then the value.

## Rulings
| Question | Ruling | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Board | 4×4, no options | spec §3.9 |
| Merges | standard: once per tile per move, nearest the move's edge first | spec §3.9; the original game's rule |
| A move that changes nothing | illegal, and spawns nothing | spec §3.9; otherwise a "move" would be a free spawn |
| Spawns | 2 at 90%, 4 at 10%; two at the start | spec §3.9; the original game's odds |
| 2048 | marked in the view and an event; play continues | spec §3.9 |
| End | no move left: a win if 2048 was reached, else a loss | spec §3.9 |
| Score | sum of merges, in the view; the archive outcome stays the solo +1/−1 | spec §3.9; the leaderboard means the same for every solo game |
| Direction input | `"up"`, `"down"`, `"left"`, `"right"`, exactly as the catalogue lists them | catalogue values are what the parser accepts |

## Other endings (core)
- Give up (`R`, confirms): a loss with no winner, reason `resign`, even after a 2048.
- Abort (`A`): only before your first move; result `aborted`, scores nothing.
- After either, `movable` is empty, because g2048's `Conclude` ends the game.

## Options
None.

## Controls and keys
Buffer-local, normal mode, in the board buffer. A direction's keys are mapped only when it would change the board.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `h` or `<Left>` | move left |
| `j` or `<Down>` | move down |
| `k` or `<Up>` | move up |
| `l` or `<Right>` | move right |
| `R` | give up |
| `A` | abort, before your first move |
| `]g` | next game waiting on your move |
| `?` | help: legend and keys |
| `q` | close |

## Display
- Each tile shows its value, centred; an empty cell is `·` (ascii `.`). The cells are checker-shaded so their edges show in tall cells.
- A panel beside the board shows `score` and the view's `goal` (2048) with `reached` or `not yet`, so reaching it never rests on colour alone. Tiles of the goal and above are also tinted with `GamesWin`.
- In a roomy window (cells 8 lines tall and up) tiles of up to four digits are drawn in block digits; larger tiles keep their one-line glyph.
- A move notifies as `alice moved left +8` (no `+` when nothing merged); the first 2048 as `alice made 2048`.

## Wire
- **Action:** `{"kind": "move", "direction": "left"}`.
- **View:** `{cells, score, goal, reached, movable}`, `goal` the tile that sets `reached`. See `docs/protocol.md` and `docs/schema/games/g2048.json`.
- **Errors:**

| Case | Message |
|---|---|
| a direction that changes nothing | `no tile can move <direction>` |
| not up, down, left or right | `want up, down, left or right; got "<text>"` |
| unknown action | `unknown action: <kind>` |
| the game is over | `not your turn` |

## Scoring and result
Win +1 (no moves left after a 2048), loss −1 (no moves left without one, or giving up), abort 0. Solo games count in the g2048 records but not in the all-games leaderboard.

## Known limits
- No undo and no board sizes other than 4×4.
- The footer lists both keys of each direction (`h,<Left> left`), so with all four directions movable it is wider than 80 columns and a narrow window cuts its end.
- Tiles of five digits and more have no block-digit art.
- The conformance transcript gives up rather than playing out, because the hub picks a random seed; playing to no moves is covered by the Go conformance and the smoke test.
